Our Educational Approach

A comprehensive curriculum designed to develop well-rounded students ready for future success.

Holistic Education for Every Child

At Leaders' Field Int'l School, we believe in developing the TOTAL CHILD. Our curriculum goes beyond traditional academics to develop physical, emotional, social, and spiritual skills that students need to thrive.

Our teaching methodology combines proven educational practices with innovative approaches, including project-based learning, collaborative activities, and technology integration. We maintain small class sizes to ensure every student receives personalized attention and support.

We follow a rigorous academic curriculum aligned with national standards while incorporating international best practices. Our dedicated teachers are continuously trained to deliver engaging, effective instruction that meets diverse learning needs.

Our Programs

Age-appropriate education for every stage of your child's development.

Ages 0-2

Creche

A safe, nurturing environment for our youngest learners. Focused on early development, sensory exploration, and building secure attachments with caring professionals.

Ages 2-5

Pre-School

Building foundational literacy and numeracy skills through structured activities and creative play. Introduction to reading, writing, and basic mathematics with focus on social development.

Grades 1-3

Lower Primary

Developing reading fluency, mathematical understanding, and scientific curiosity. Students build essential skills in communication and critical thinking.

Grades 4-6

Upper Primary

Advanced academic curriculum preparing students for secondary education. Focus on independent learning, research skills, and leadership development.
