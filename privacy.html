Privacy Policy

Home / Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use our Contact form, we collect the information you choose to provide, which may include your first and last name, email address, phone number, the subject of your inquiry, your child's grade level (if applicable), and your message. If you tick the newsletter checkbox, we also note your consent to receive future updates by email.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you submit solely to respond to your enquiry, process admissions requests, and, where you have opted in, to send you school news and updates. We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

3. How Your Information Is Processed

Our contact form is delivered using Web3Forms, a third-party form processing service, which forwards your submission to our school email address. We do not store form submissions in our own database beyond our email inbox.

4. Children's Information

Our admissions and contact processes may involve information about prospective pupils (such as a child's grade level) provided to us by a parent or guardian. We only use this information to process admissions enquiries and do not publish it publicly.

5. Data Retention

We retain enquiry information only as long as necessary to respond to you and process any related admissions request, or until you ask us to delete it.

6. Your Rights

In line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time by contacting us using the details below.

7. Contact Us
