Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about admissions.

When does the academic year begin?

Our academic year runs on a three-term system: First Term (September – December), Second Term (January – April), and Third Term (April/May – July), with breaks between terms.

What is the class size?

We maintain small class sizes with a maximum of 20 students per class. This ensures personalized attention and effective learning for every student.

Do you provide transportation?

Yes, we offer school bus service covering major areas of the city. Transportation fees vary based on distance. Please contact our office for routes and pricing.

Is there an entrance exam?

For Creche and Pre-School, we conduct a simple readiness assessment. For Primary levels, students take a placement assessment in English and Mathematics to determine appropriate class placement.

Can I visit the school before applying?

Absolutely! We encourage all prospective families to schedule a campus visit. You can tour our facilities, meet our staff, and observe classes in session. Contact us to book your visit.
