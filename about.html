Our Story

Raising leaders who impact their generation positively.

Leaders' Field Int'l School was founded with a powerful vision: to be one of the foremost, outstanding and value-based schools with a strong sense of ethical responsibility geared towards developing the TOTAL CHILD.

We are committed to high quality education that delivers transformative learning to all pupils. We nurture individual talents and emphasize the importance of developing children physically, emotionally, socially and spiritually.

Our approach combines academic excellence with character building, ensuring that our students develop into leaders who will impact their generation positively. We offer comprehensive programs from Creche through Pre-School to Primary education.

Message from the Principal

"At Leaders' Field Int'l School, we believe every child has unlimited potential. Our role is to nurture the TOTAL CHILD - developing them physically, emotionally, socially and spiritually to become leaders who impact their generation."

Welcome to Leaders' Field Int'l School. As the Principal, I am honored to lead a team of dedicated educators who share a common passion for transformative education.

Our school is more than just a place of learning – it is a community where students, parents, and teachers work together to raise leaders. We understand that education must develop the TOTAL CHILD, and we take this responsibility seriously.

We are committed to providing an education that develops not only academic skills but also physical, emotional, social and spiritual abilities. Our students learn to think critically, communicate effectively, and live with integrity – becoming leaders who will impact their generation positively.

I invite you to visit our school and experience firsthand the transformative atmosphere that makes Leaders' Field Int'l School special. Together, let's raise your child to be a leader.
