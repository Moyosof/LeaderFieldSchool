Our Story

Raising leaders who impact their generation positively.

Leaders' Field Int'l School was founded with a powerful vision: to be one of the foremost, outstanding and value-based schools with a strong sense of ethical responsibility geared towards developing the TOTAL CHILD.

We are committed to high quality education that delivers transformative learning to all pupils. We nurture individual talents and emphasize the importance of developing children physically, emotionally, socially and spiritually.

Our approach combines academic excellence with character building, ensuring that our students develop into leaders who will impact their generation positively. We offer comprehensive programs from Creche through Pre-School to Primary education, delivered on a British-curriculum framework adapted to the Nigerian context.
